# *Kefu* coding: codebook and counts

*Kefu* (克夫, literally "to doom one's husband") is a folk belief that a woman's fate brings death to her husbands. The film never uses the word; viewers bring it in.

**Selection.** Every long review and short comment containing 克夫: 10 of 306 long reviews (two repeat the same text, so 9 distinct) and 12 of 1,079 short comments, for 21 distinct texts. Passages were exported by the R Markdown file (sections 3–6) and coded by hand.

**Unit.** The whole review or comment, read in full.

**Codes.**

| Code | Definition |
|---|---|
| Apply | States the label, even as a joke or a plot description |
| Question | Raises *kefu* as a possibility and never resolves it |
| Era | Raises *kefu* only as a question or an earlier assumption, and states that the era caused the deaths |
| Critique | Criticizes the belief itself: what it does, especially to women |
| Other | None of the above |

**Counts.**

| Code | Long reviews | Short comments | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Apply | 2 | 9 | 11 |
| Era | 4 | 1 | 5 |
| Critique | 2 | 1 | 3 |
| Question | 1 | 0 | 1 |
| Other | 0 | 1 | 1 |
| Total | 9 | 12 | 21 |

**Note.** A joke that applies the label shows that the belief is familiar, not that the commenter holds it. Review texts and per-text codes are not redistributed, to protect reviewers' privacy.
