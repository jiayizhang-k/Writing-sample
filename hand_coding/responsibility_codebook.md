# Responsibility coding: codebook and counts

**Selection.** Long reviews (of 306) containing at least one word for blame or for an agent, such as 责任 (responsibility), 是谁 (who), 领袖 (leader), 毛泽东 (Mao Zedong), and 人为 (man-made): 75 reviews, 74 distinct texts (near-duplicates by one author counted once).

**Unit.** The whole review, read in full.

**Rules.**
- Each review receives one code for where it places the cause of the suffering: Politics, Fate, or Neither.
- "The era" counts as Fate when described as a force (e.g., a torrent) and as Politics when accused.
- Retelling the film's events does not count as an attribution unless the reviewer names a cause the film does not show.
- Flags are recorded independently of the main code; a review coded Politics may also use the language of fate.

**Note.** Because the reviews were selected by keyword, counts are minimums for the 306 long reviews, not proportions. Review texts and per-review codes are not redistributed, to protect reviewers' privacy.
